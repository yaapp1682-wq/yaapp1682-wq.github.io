PigeonSim Map — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This page explains how the iOS app "PigeonSim Map" (the "App") handles information.

Information collected by the developer

The developer does not collect personal information. The App has no accounts and does not send data to the developer's servers (the developer does not operate any servers). Records, pigeon upgrades and settings are stored only on your device.

Location

Only when you choose "Fly from current location" in Free Flight, the App gets your current location with your permission through iOS. It is used on the device only to decide where to start flying and is never sent to the developer. You can still play without allowing it (search for a place or pick one on the map).

Maps and place information

The App uses the following external services. To display content, the coordinates you are viewing and the names of places are sent to these services.

  • Apple Maps (MapKit, Look Around): maps, 3D cities, place search and routes. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Maps (Street View): when Street View is shown in place details. Google Privacy Policy
  • Wikipedia / Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation): place descriptions and finding nearby sights. Wikimedia Privacy Policy

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads by Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). To serve ads, Google may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and device information.

  • The App shows the iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. You can use the App even if you choose "Ask App Not to Track" (non-personalized ads will be shown).
  • In the UK, Switzerland, the European Economic Area (EEA) and similar regions, the App shows a consent form by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time from "Ad Privacy Settings" in the Settings screen.
  • For how Google handles information, see the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps and AdMob data disclosure.

If you buy Premium (a one-time in-app purchase through the App Store), no ads are shown and the ad-related data collection above does not take place.

Game Center

For leaderboards, the App sends scores (deliveries and distance) to Apple's Game Center. Game Center account information (such as nicknames) is managed by Apple and is not obtained by the developer. If you are not signed in to Game Center, no scores are sent.

Purchases

Purchases of Premium are processed by the App Store (Apple). The developer never obtains payment information such as credit card details.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as features are added or laws change. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

Please contact us via the support page.
