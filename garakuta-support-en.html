Junk Drop — Support

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Thank you for playing "Junk Drop". Here is how to play and answers to common questions.

How to play

  • Drag left and right to choose where the falling part lands. Parts that touch your core stick to it.
  • Drop parts you don't need away from the core — they stay on the ground as junk.
  • When you're ready, tap "Challenge!" and let physics do the rest.
  • New players should start with "Part Practice" on the title screen.

FAQ

Q. I bought Premium, but it's gone after changing devices

Tap "Premium" at the top right of the title screen, then tap "Restore Purchase". With the same Apple Account, Premium comes back for free.

Q. What does Premium include?

All ads are removed, and you can change your core's color and face with "Core Skins". Endless Runs always start with 4 lives (3 in the free version, or 4 after watching an ad).

Q. My score doesn't show up on the leaderboard

Leaderboards use Apple Game Center. Please sign in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone before playing.

Q. I want to change my ad consent (privacy) settings

In regions such as the EEA, a "Privacy settings" button appears at the bottom of the title screen, where you can change your choice at any time. Tracking permission can be changed under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
