Junk Drop — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This page explains how the iOS app "Junk Drop" (ガラクタドロップ, the "App") handles information.

Information collected by the developer

The developer does not collect any personal information. The App has no account registration and never sends data to the developer's servers (the developer runs no servers). Game progress, best records and settings are stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads). To serve ads, Google may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and device information.

  • The App asks for tracking permission via iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT). You can still use the App if you choose "Ask App Not to Track" (non-personalized ads are shown).
  • In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and similar regions, the App asks for consent using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy settings" on the title screen.
  • For how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services and AdMob data collection.

If you buy Premium (a one-time in-app purchase through the App Store), no ads are shown and none of the ad-related data collection above takes place.

Game Center

For leaderboards, the App sends your scores to Apple's Game Center. Game Center account information (such as your nickname) is managed by Apple and is not collected by the developer. Scores are not sent if you are not signed in to Game Center.

Saving videos (replays)

The replay video feature uses iOS ReplayKit to record only the App's screen. Recording happens only when you tap the save button, and saving and sharing are done through the standard iOS screen. Videos are never sent to the developer.

Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by the App Store (Apple). The developer never receives payment information such as credit card details.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features or laws change. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact
