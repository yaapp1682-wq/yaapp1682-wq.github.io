PigeonSim Map — Support

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Thank you for playing "PigeonSim Map". Here is how to play and answers to common questions.

How to play

  • Tilt the stick left/right to turn, up to flap and climb, and down to descend. Use Boost to go faster and Brake to slow down.
  • Carrier Pigeon: deliver letters until your stamina runs out on five stages that change every day. Flying high drains stamina faster; resting on the ground recovers a little.
  • World Sightseeing: pick a region and fly around its sights. Get close to a marker and a "View" button appears with details about the place.
  • Free Flight: fly freely from your current location, a searched place or a spot picked on the map.

FAQ

Q. I bought Premium, but it was reset after changing devices

Tap "Premium" at the top right of the title screen, then "Restore Purchase". With the same Apple Account, Premium is restored for free.

Q. What does Premium include?

All ads are removed, and the parts that normally require watching an ad (such as replaying the same stage) can be played without ads. You can also choose pigeon colors (white, black).

Q. My score doesn't appear in the leaderboard

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Please sign in from Settings → Game Center on your iPhone before playing.

Q. Some places don't show 3D buildings

3D cities are shown only where Apple Maps supports 3D. Carrier Pigeon stages are chosen from 3D-supported cities.

Q. I want to change my ad consent (privacy) settings

In the UK, Europe and similar regions, "Ad Privacy Settings" appears in the Settings screen, where you can change it at any time. Tracking permission can be changed in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
